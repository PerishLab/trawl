# Architecture

## Purpose

Trawl is perish.code's independent closure for directed acquisition and search
over the external world.

Its long-term direction combines:

```text
small Google-shaped discovery and search
  x acquisition beyond HTTP
  constrained by directed perish.code needs
```

The third line governs construction. Google describes an evolutionary ceiling,
not a Day 0 implementation checklist.

## Independent closure

Trawl must remain useful without Noema or any other consumer. It owns its
operational continuity and exposes material for several consumers without
making one consumer's vocabulary the producer contract.

Noema may absorb selected material into a thought topology. Search, alerts,
archives, BI, publishing, or Santi souls may consume other projections. Those
consumers do not become Trawl modules.

## Construction law

Every new capability must close at least one enumerable acquisition scenario
inside perish.code.

Scenario-specific behavior stays local until repeated consumers prove a stable
mechanism. Support for another protocol, adapter, index, ranking method,
scheduler, or distribution strategy does not exist merely because a large
search company has one.

The first scenario must close the smallest useful loop:

```text
directed need
  -> acquisition
  -> searchable material
  -> consumption
  -> feedback into later acquisition
```

The cold-start discussion will decide the minimum atoms and capability boundary
for that loop.

## Deferred until scenario pressure

- Source, Attempt, Observation, Artifact, and discovery identities;
- corpus, indexing, query, ranking, and change semantics;
- HTTP, browser, API, Git, feed, filesystem, and other adapters;
- scheduling, frontier, retry, credentials, budgets, and politeness;
- consumer delivery, replay, subscriptions, and checkpoints;
- binaries, APIs, storage, deployment, release, and user interfaces.
