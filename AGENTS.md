# Agent guide

Trawl is the independent directed acquisition and search closure for
perish.code.

## Current stage

The repository contains only its Plumb-governed skeleton and the empty `trawl`
library anchor at `crates/trawl`.

- Mini Google describes the long-term information-loop direction, not a Day 0
  feature list.
- Collection must be able to grow beyond HTTP, but adapters appear only under
  real scenario pressure.
- Enumerable perish.code acquisition needs authorize construction and constrain
  generalization.
- Noema is one consumer. Trawl does not depend on Noema or shape its own
  contract around Noema Media and Relation vocabulary.
- Source, Attempt, Observation, Artifact, corpus, index, ranking, scheduler,
  adapter, and consumer semantics are not settled yet.
- No binary, API, release, deployment, or ingestion surface exists.
- Product vocabulary and laws remain local; repository mechanism remains
  Plumb's right to amend.

## Stable boundary

Trawl owns the path from directed external acquisition pressure toward a
searchable, consumable information surface. It must remain independently useful
when every downstream consumer is offline.

Consumers own what acquired material means in their domains. Trawl must not
absorb Noema topology, Santi cognition, publishing workflows, BI presentation,
or downstream action semantics merely because they consume its output.

See `docs/architecture.md` for the complete pre-model boundary.
